**Biblical Exegesis and Theological Inquiry**

This guide explains the standpoint behind the archive’s articles, reports, posters and interactive studies. It also explains how readers can use them.

## 1. About the project

This personal research archive aims to deepen understanding of biblical texts and encourage sound theological inquiry. I use a range of scholarly tools to examine difficult passages and share the reasoning behind my interpretations. Through that work, I hope to strengthen the intellectual foundations of Christian faith.

I aim to offer coherent arguments that readers can check against the evidence. **I aim for the rigor expected in peer review. Any independent expert review a particular study received will be identified in that study**, including who reviewed it, what they examined and when.

## 2. About the author and my standpoint

**Faith background.** I am a lay member of the Presbyterian Church in Korea (Hapdong). My theological perspective is rooted in this tradition.

**Whose views appear here.** I write in a personal capacity. The archive does not speak for my church, denomination or any other organization. I distinguish views described in the scholarly literature from my own conclusions.

**Motivation.** This is a voluntary inquiry into questions of faith that arise as I try to understand Scripture more deeply and accurately. No outside body commissioned the project, and its purpose is open inquiry rather than advocacy for a predetermined conclusion.

## 3. Research and citation

**Approach to interpretation.** I work within the Reformed tradition. My primary method is historical-grammatical interpretation, with close attention to meaning in the original language and context. I also consider literary structure, historical and social setting, connections across the canon, and reception history. Each approach has its own questions and limitations.

**Primary sources.** Scripture holds primary and final authority for faith and interpretation in this project. Studies identify the original-language edition, reliable translations and specific passages used. I distinguish direct quotations from translations and paraphrases I supply for explanation.

**Scholarly sources.** I give priority to reliable journals, commentaries, scholarly books and dissertations from Korea and elsewhere. Where possible, I provide the author, title, publication year, page and DOI or link. I state when I have seen only an abstract or a quotation in another work.

**Other viewpoints.** A source may differ from my position or advance a disputed claim. **Citation alone does not indicate agreement, endorsement, recommendation or affiliation.** I first explain the source’s own reasons, then identify my assessment or objections. When an online source has limited reliability, I explain the limitation.

**Claims and evidence.** I distinguish direct textual observations, interpretations grounded in the literature, scholarly disputes and my own inferences. I identify minority views as such and check the attribution before assigning a claim to a particular scholar.

## 4. Scope and limits

**Qualifications.** I am not a credentialed theologian, pastor or Bible teacher. This archive cannot replace professional theological education or pastoral counseling.

**Limits of the material.** I aim for careful and accurate research, but cannot guarantee completeness or freedom from error. I correct mistranslations, bibliographic errors, interpretive biases and omissions when identified, and record significant changes.

**Readers’ judgment.** Readers should review the material and decide how to interpret and apply it. Consult the leaders of your own church about important matters of faith. The archive is not a final authority for spiritual or pastoral guidance. I remain responsible for accurate citation and corrections. These conditions do not limit readers’ legal rights or exclude liability that the law does not permit anyone to exclude.

## 5. Participation and discussion

Constructive feedback and scholarly debate are welcome. Please treat people of differing religious and academic convictions with respect. Where possible, support a criticism or counterargument with the passage, its context, the primary source and your reasoning.

Personal attacks, ridicule, hate speech, disclosure of private information and unsubstantiated accusations against groups or individuals are prohibited. I may hide or remove such material and spam in discussions I moderate. Disagreement with my position is welcome.

Where comments are unavailable, use the contact channels listed on the About page. Please keep personal contact details and sensitive matters of faith or counseling out of public messages. [About](/en/about/)

When quoting portions of my work for personal, noncommercial research or education, identify the author, article title and original link. For republication in full, redistribution, commercial use, translation or publication of an edited version, please contact me in writing beforehand, unless the use is already permitted by law or a separate license. ## 7. AI assistance and visualizations

I may use generative AI to find materials, assist with translation, draft and organize text, and write code. I retain responsibility for publication, references and fact-checking. AI output does not establish scholarly evidence or authority. I identify AI review separately from expert peer review.

Dashboard scores, graphs and simulations need an explanation of how they were produced. I label illustrative examples and my own qualitative assessments. **Unsupported percentages must not be presented as scholarly agreement, objective probabilities of a textual reconstruction or actual reader reactions.** A disclaimer cannot verify a figure or bibliographic entry.

## 8. Corrections

If you find an error, send the article URL, the passage or sentence in question and evidence I can check. After review, I will decide whether to correct, supplement or withdraw the material. I record the date and reason for changes affecting a conclusion.
