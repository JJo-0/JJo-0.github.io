This is a personal archive of questions from technology, research and study. With the addition of **Biblical Studies** to Writing, I want to explain the principles that guide how I create and use articles, reports, posters, code, images and interactive material across the blog.

## 1. Introducing Biblical Exegesis and Theological Inquiry

**Biblical Exegesis and Theological Inquiry** is a personal research project. I read passages closely, examine their original languages, context, historical background and theological questions, and document the reasons behind interpretations.

I am a lay member of the Presbyterian Church in Korea (Hapdong). My primary approach is historical-grammatical interpretation within the Reformed tradition. I undertake this work voluntarily to understand the biblical text more deeply and accurately, with room for a passage to challenge my expectations.

The reading guide introduces my theological standpoint. The archive and the Biblical Studies section of Writing collect public material. This notice does not announce independent review of any particular study or publication of all the underlying manuscripts. [Reading guide](/en/bible/reading-guide/) · [Biblical Studies archive](/en/bible/) · [Biblical Studies in Writing](/en/posts/#biblical-studies)

## 2. Authorship and institutional positions

Interpretations and conclusions on this blog reflect my own research and judgment. I do not speak for my university, laboratory, institution, church, denomination or any company or organization mentioned here. When I cite official material, I distinguish what the source says from my commentary.

A citation of a denomination, scholar, researcher or company does not imply agreement, endorsement, affiliation or sponsorship. Where views differ, I present the reasons their proponents give and identify my own assessment and objections separately.

## 3. Evidence, interpretation and verification

I distinguish statements found directly in Scripture or another primary source from a source’s interpretation, a disputed hypothesis or my own inference. I provide sources readers can check and the date or period they cover. Where I have seen only an abstract or a quotation in another work, I identify that limitation.

I aim for scholarly rigor and report expert review, journal publication, experimental validation and safety certification only when they have occurred. **An AI review or self-assigned score does not constitute independent expert peer review.** I label illustrative graphs and qualitative judgments so readers can distinguish them from measurements, estimates of scholarly support and objective probabilities.

These principles guide future editing and corrections. Verification varies across existing posts. Please consult each article’s sources, scope of verification and correction history.

## 4. AI assistance and author responsibility

I may use generative AI to explore sources, assist with translation, draft and edit text, write code and create visualizations. I check that a source exists and that it supports the relevant claim. AI output by itself does not establish that a claim is true.

I disclose the extent of AI assistance where it matters to interpretation. I make the final publication decision and remain responsible for correcting errors, including errors introduced by AI.

## 5. Copyright and reuse

Rights in my writing, analysis and diagrams are distinct from rights in external materials. When quoting portions of my work for noncommercial personal research or education, credit the author, give the article title and link to the original.

For full republication, redistribution, commercial use, translation or publication of an edited version, contact me in writing beforehand unless the use is already permitted by law or a separate license. Bible translations, figures from papers, photographs, data and external code each have their own rights and terms. **Attribution alone does not permit every form of copying or reuse.**

Existing open-source licenses and permissions remain in effect. This notice does not retroactively withdraw or restrict them, or limit rights such as quotation granted by law. [Copyright, quotation and reuse](/en/policies/copyright/)

## 6. Limits on use

Investment, financial, health, legal and tax material cannot replace professional advice on an individual case. Investment outlooks and backtests cannot guarantee future returns. Reports on health and biomedical research do not provide diagnoses or prescriptions.

Check the stated environment and conditions before using code or applying material on AI, robotics or equipment, and validate it for the intended use. Educational examples and simulations cannot replace on-site safety approval or product certification. The biblical studies material cannot replace professional theological education or pastoral counseling.

Readers should check and use the material carefully. I retain responsibility for accurate quotation and correction. This notice does not seek a blanket exemption from liability that the law does not allow anyone to exclude. [Disclaimers by subject](/en/policies/disclaimer/)

## 7. Feedback and corrections

I welcome criticism backed by clear evidence and views that differ from my own. In spaces I moderate, I may restrict personal attacks, unfounded accusations, hate speech, exposure of private information and spam. A difference of opinion is not grounds for excluding criticism.

When I verify a significant error, I consider correction, clarification or withdrawal. I record the date and reason for changes that affect a conclusion. To report an error or a rights issue, identify the page, the exact location and evidence I can check. Keep sensitive counseling details, account information and private evidence of ownership out of public posts. Contact channels appear on the About page. [About](/en/about/)
