## Personal writing and institutional positions

Except where an official statement is explicitly quoted, interpretations and conclusions are the author’s own. They do not speak for the author’s university, laboratory, employer, church or denomination, or for a company mentioned here. A reference to a logo, product, paper or researcher does not imply affiliation, endorsement or a guarantee.

## Sources, dates and scope of verification

Articles distinguish documented facts, a source’s claims, the author’s inferences and illustrative examples. They prioritize verifiable primary sources such as papers, official documentation and filings. Publication dates, event dates and the dates underlying a dataset serve different purposes. If only an abstract or a secondary quotation was available, the article should say so.

Publication of this policy does not establish that all older articles have been checked again. Prices, laws, specifications, software versions and research findings change. Consult the current original source before applying them.

## AI assistance

Generative AI may assist with research, translation, drafting, editing, code and figures. The author must check that cited sources exist and support the claims attributed to them. AI output alone cannot substantiate a claim.

The author decides what to publish and remains responsible for corrections. Reports must describe the experiments, browser tests and expert reviews actually performed. AI self-ratings and cross-checks between models do not establish independent peer review, safety certification or objective accuracy. Articles should disclose material AI contributions.

## Images, graphs and numbers

External images and figures should identify the source and rights holder where possible. Reconstructed diagrams, synthetic illustrations, example data and simulation results should be labeled so readers can distinguish them from original figures, photographs and measurements. Figures for scholarly support, confidence and evaluation scores require a source or a calculation method. Unverified numbers should not be presented as measured data.

## Advertising, sponsorship and interests

The site may display advertising. Automatically served ads do not express an endorsement or a guarantee of safety. Articles should identify sponsorship, affiliate links, supplied products and other financial interests that could affect a reader’s judgment. Check the individual disclosure on investment articles. This policy does not certify the absence of interests across the archive.

## External links and analytics

External sites set their own terms and may change their content, access requirements or links. Features such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense may process cookies and connection data. See [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

This page does not replace a provider’s privacy policy or any required consent process. Guidance will be updated to reflect new comment or external services actually used.

## Discussion, corrections and rights inquiries

Evidence-based criticism and differing interpretations are welcome. The author may hide or remove personal attacks, hate speech, disclosures of personal information, spam and unsupported defamatory claims in spaces under the author’s control. Disagreement alone is no reason to remove a comment.

Include the article URL, the passage, image or code involved, and verifiable evidence in a report. Contact details are on the [About page](/en/about/). Keep national identification numbers, unpublished research, account information and private proof of rights out of public threads.

## Revision history and application

Substantial changes to facts, conclusions or citations should record the date and reason. Where evidence warrants it, the author will correct, supplement or withdraw material, including evidence that changes an earlier conclusion.
