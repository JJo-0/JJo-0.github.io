## 1. Who owns the work?

Unless otherwise indicated, the author holds rights in original writing, analyses, and charts to the extent that the law protects them. I claim no exclusive rights in facts, ideas, public-domain material, or third-party works in which I hold no rights.

Bible translations, extracts from books and papers, tables, figures, photographs, logos, fonts, data, and software belong to their respective rights holders. The blog’s copyright notice neither transfers their rights to me nor grants permission to reuse their work.

## 2. Quoting original content

When quoting excerpts from my work for noncommercial personal research or education, clearly credit **the author and article title, and link to the original**. Where possible, include the publication or revision date. Distinguish quotations from your commentary and disclose any abridgment, translation, or adaptation so readers can recognize the original meaning.

Readers retain the benefit of statutory exceptions and limitations, including lawful quotation and fair use. Whether a particular use qualifies depends on the use and the applicable law. **Attribution alone does not authorize every reproduction or republication.**

## 3. Republication, redistribution, and commercial use

Unless the law or a separate license permits the use, please contact me in writing before reproducing a whole article, redistributing files, using material commercially, publishing a translation or edited version, or incorporating content into a product. Include the source URL, the part you wish to use, the medium, your purpose, and any proposed changes. Contact details are on the [About page](/en/about/).

I can grant permission only for material in which I hold rights. For example, reusing a figure from a cited paper or a passage from a translation requires checking the relevant rights holder’s conditions. A license attached to a specific work and permission already granted take precedence over this general guide.

## 4. Bible translations, critical editions, and commentaries

Ancient biblical texts, modern translations, editorial material in critical editions, and commentaries have distinct rights considerations. My practice is to check the translation and edition, passages quoted, rights holder, and required attribution. I identify my own explanatory translations and paraphrases so they cannot be mistaken for quotations from a published Bible translation.

The Korean Bible Society provides rights and usage guidance for its Korean translations. Protection varies by translation. Each translation’s own terms apply. A Bible-reading service that supplies the full text, file distribution, and quotation of excerpts are different uses.

For guidance checked on September 11, 2026, see the [Korean Bible Society copyright guide](https://www.bskorea.or.kr/bbs/content.php?co_id=subpage2_3_4_1) and [copyright FAQ](https://www.bskorea.or.kr/bbs/board.php?bo_table=copyright_faq). For translations and source editions published by others, consult the relevant rights holder.

## 5. Open-source code and site software

The applicable repository `LICENSE` and third-party notices govern the site theme, software, and associated documentation. **Existing open-source licenses, including MIT, retain their full effect.** For code blocks or external code carrying a separate license, check that license first.

An open-source release of the software does not place every article, external image, and Bible translation under the same license. The noncommercial guidance for blog content does not restrict commercial use of code where that use has already been expressly permitted. Previously granted licenses remain in effect.

## 6. AI-generated and reconstructed material

I distinguish AI-generated material from parts that I creatively edited, selected, or arranged. AI use by itself does not establish exclusive rights in every output. Redrawing a figure or altering an image with AI still requires a review of the rights in the source.

## 7. Rights issues and correction requests

If you suspect an infringement, please identify the page, the material and its location, the rights holder, and verifiable evidence. Depending on the facts, I will consider improving attribution, replacing the material, restricting access, or removing it. Publishing a notice does not resolve a rights issue or authorize the use of another person’s work.

Keep personal information and private documents establishing rights out of public comments and issues. Contact details are on the [About page](/en/about/).

## 8. Application and references

This page explains how to use the blog. It does not provide legal advice for individual cases. Applicable law and work-specific licenses take precedence. The [Copyright Act at the Korean National Law Information Center](https://www.law.go.kr/법령/저작권법) and the [Korea Copyright Commission](https://www.copyright.or.kr/) provide further reference material.
