## General guidance

Articles, reports, code and diagrams share the author’s learning and research. The author cannot guarantee completeness, accuracy, suitability for a particular purpose or continuing availability of external services. Check the article dates, data dates, sources and conditions.

Readers should assess material before using it. This notice preserves the author’s responsibility for accurate citation, fact-checking and corrections. It does not limit rights or exclude liability where the law does not allow it.

## Investing, finance and company analysis

Writing on companies, industries, securities, cryptoassets and automated trading provides general analysis. It does not offer individual investment advice or recommend buying or selling a particular asset. Targets, forecasts and scenarios are estimates. Past performance, backtests and paper trading do not guarantee future returns.

Fees, taxes, slippage, liquidity, failed fills and data bias can change outcomes. Investing can lead to loss of principal. Consult current filings, official sources and qualified advice where appropriate. Verify code before connecting it to a live account. Legal obligations depend on the actual content and activity, regardless of how this notice describes them.

## Health, nutrition and biomedical research

Articles on health, illness, nutrition and supplements provide general information. Diagnosis, prescribing and treatment require individual clinical judgment. Cell and animal studies and early trials do not establish a treatment effect in people.

Discuss symptoms, medicines and allergies with a qualified clinician. Do not start, change or stop prescribed treatment solely on the basis of an article.

## Code, AI, robots and equipment

Examples depend on the specified software, hardware and experimental conditions. Operation, security, performance and reproducibility in other settings require testing. Review dependencies, permissions, security, backups and the possibility of data loss. Test in an isolated environment first.

Deployment on a robot, machine or experimental process requires risk assessment and independent safety verification. A paper, simulation or blog explanation cannot replace product certification, regulatory assessment or on-site safety approval.

## Research, news, law and tax

Separate the original researchers’ claims from the author’s commentary. Preprints, hypotheses and limited experiments do not by themselves establish scholarly consensus. Read company announcements and news in light of the evidence available on their publication date.

Legal or tax explanations provide general information. For personal decisions, check applicable law and official guidance and obtain professional review as needed. This notice offers no legal opinion about its effect in a particular dispute.

## Biblical and theological studies

This section records a layperson’s research and does not speak for a church or denomination. It cannot replace formal theological education, pastoral counsel or personal discernment on important matters of faith. The [reading guide](/en/bible/reading-guide/) explains the author’s standpoint, source selection and discussion policy.

## External materials and rights

Providers set the terms for external sites, advertisements, third-party material and software. The blog cannot guarantee the results of using an external service. Before reusing material, check its license and the [copyright and reuse policy](/en/policies/copyright/).
