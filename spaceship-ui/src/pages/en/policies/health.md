This guide applies to Health & Lifestyle in Writing and to health, nutrition, and life sciences research in News, including related reports, charts, and product comparisons. Read it alongside the [editorial and AI policies](/en/policies/), [terms of use and disclaimer](/en/policies/disclaimer/), and [copyright and reuse guide](/en/policies/copyright/).

## 1. Personal research and medical decisions

These articles record my research and study and share general information. I speak for myself. My university, laboratory, and employer, and any medical institutions, professional societies, and companies mentioned, have their own views. Citing a paper or opinion does not endorse its conclusions, guarantee a treatment effect, or recommend a product.

This material cannot diagnose an individual, prescribe treatment, or replace care and advice from a medical professional. This guide does not waive duties that apply to the actual content or conduct.

## 2. Research findings and their scope

I prioritize verifiable sources such as papers, official guidelines, and product labels. My practice is to identify the population or samples studied, the study conditions, the writing or revision date, and what I verified. Cell and animal studies, observational research, and clinical trials provide different levels of evidence.

Early findings and small samples do not establish a treatment effect in humans. I identify claims made by the paper, my interpretation, and hypotheses that still need testing. I disclose when I have seen only an abstract or a secondary citation. Publication of this guide does not certify that every earlier article has been rechecked.

## 3. Using health information and product comparisons

Discuss decisions about your symptoms, medications, or allergies with an appropriate health professional. Do not start, change, or stop treatment or a prescription solely on the basis of a blog article. If you need medical care, seek it without waiting for a reply here.

Ingredient comparisons and personal experiences with foods, supplements, or household products cannot guarantee results for an individual or prescribe a treatment. Check the product specifications, conditions of use, and precautions that apply to you. A testimonial, a single study, and an advertising claim provide different kinds of evidence.

## 4. AI, advertising, and sponsorship disclosures

I may use generative AI to find materials, assist with translation, draft text, or create visualizations. AI responses provide neither medical evidence nor expert review. I check how each source supports the associated claim and take responsibility for publication decisions and corrections.

My practice is to disclose financial interests that could materially affect a reader’s judgment in the relevant article, including free products, sponsorships, writing fees, and affiliate links. An automatically displayed advertisement conveys neither my recommendation nor a guarantee of a product or service’s safety or effectiveness.

## 5. Copyright, quotation, and reuse

Rights in my original work are separate from rights in the papers, clinical guidelines, external photographs, figures, data, and product images I cite. Third-party material remains subject to the respective rights holder’s terms.

For excerpts from my work used in noncommercial personal research or education, credit **the author and article title, and link to the original**. Please contact me in writing before reproducing a whole work, redistributing files, using material commercially, or publishing a translation or edited version, unless the law or a separate license already permits the use. Attribution alone does not authorize every reproduction or republication.

Individual licenses, open-source terms, existing permissions, and readers’ statutory rights of quotation continue to apply. AI-generated and reconstructed material also requires a review of the rights in its sources. See the [copyright and reuse guide](/en/policies/copyright/).

## 6. Errors, rights inquiries, and privacy

I welcome substantiated criticism and correction requests. I record the date and reason when a correction changes a material fact, interpretation, or conclusion. If a rights issue is confirmed, I will consider improving attribution, replacing the material, restricting access, or removing it. Include the article URL, the specific passage, and verifiable evidence. Contact details are on the [About page](/en/about/).

Keep sensitive personal information, including resident registration numbers, medical records, and test results, and private documents establishing rights out of public comments and issues. Public discussion boards cannot provide individual medical care or consultation.

## 7. Scope of responsibility

I give no guarantee that material is complete, error-free, or suitable for a particular person, or that an external service will remain available. Please use the material carefully. I remain responsible for accurate attribution, fact-checking, corrections, and any liability that the law does not allow me to exclude. This document offers no legal opinion on an individual case.
