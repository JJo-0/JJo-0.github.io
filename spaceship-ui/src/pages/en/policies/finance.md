This guide applies to Finance & Industry in Writing and to investment, company, and industry coverage in News. It covers articles, reports, charts, and automated trading examples. Read it alongside the [editorial and AI policies](/en/policies/), [terms of use and disclaimer](/en/policies/disclaimer/), and [copyright and reuse guide](/en/policies/copyright/).

## 1. Personal research and investment advice

These articles record my research and study using public sources. I speak for myself. My university, laboratory, employer, and the companies and financial institutions mentioned have their own views. Citing a researcher or analyst does not imply agreement with or endorsement of their conclusions.

The material provides neither investment advice tailored to anyone’s finances and objectives nor a recommendation to buy or sell a specific asset. The actual content and conduct determine which legal duties apply. The title or wording of this guide cannot exclude those duties.

## 2. Facts, forecasts, and verification

I distinguish facts documented in filings, company announcements, and primary materials from the sources’ claims, my estimates, and illustrative scenarios. For prices, exchange rates, and financial figures, I aim to identify the reference date, units, and available sources. I disclose the limits when I cannot verify a source directly.

Price targets, potential gains, estimates about customers, and industry forecasts involve uncertainty. Check the latest official sources before a transaction, as an article may predate a new filing or a change in market conditions. Publication of this guide does not certify that every earlier article has been rechecked.

## 3. Investment risks and automated trading

Past performance, backtests, and paper trading cannot guarantee future returns. Fees, taxes, slippage, liquidity, failed executions, and data bias can all change the outcome. Real investments carry a risk of losing principal.

Trading code and settings are educational examples. I make no guarantee about their operation, security, or profitability in an environment I have not expressly identified as tested. Verify the example before connecting a live account, including its data, order handling, permissions, and loss controls. Keep account information, API keys, and authentication details out of public comments and issues.

## 4. AI, advertising, and financial interests

I may use generative AI to find materials, assist with translation, draft text, write code, or produce visualizations. I check citations and the meaning of numerical claims independently. AI output alone does not establish a fact. I am responsible for publication decisions and corrections.

My practice is to disclose financial interests that could materially affect a reader’s judgment, including asset holdings, sponsorships, writing fees, and affiliate links, in the relevant article. I make no blanket assurance that every article is free of such interests. Automatically displayed advertisements convey neither my recommendation nor a guarantee of returns.

## 5. Copyright, quotation, and reuse

Rights in my original writing, analyses, and charts are separate from rights in filings, brokerage reports, external charts, data, images, and software. Mentioning third-party material here does not grant permission to redistribute it.

For excerpts from my work used in noncommercial personal research or education, credit **the author and article title, and link to the original**. Please contact me in writing before reproducing a whole work, redistributing files, using material commercially, or publishing a translation or edited version, unless the law or a separate license already permits the use. Check the rights holder’s terms for third-party material.

Existing open-source licenses, including MIT, and permissions already granted remain in force. Readers retain rights such as quotation permitted by law. See the [copyright and reuse guide](/en/policies/copyright/).

## 6. Corrections, rights inquiries, and responsibility

I welcome substantiated criticism and error reports. I record the date and reason when a correction changes a material fact, calculation, or conclusion. If a rights issue is confirmed, I will consider improving attribution, replacing the material, restricting access, or removing it. Include the article URL, the specific passage, and verifiable evidence. Contact details are on the [About page](/en/about/).

I give no guarantee that the material is complete, error-free, or suitable for a particular investment objective. Please review it independently. I remain responsible for accurate attribution, fact-checking, corrections, and any liability that the law does not allow me to exclude. This document offers no legal or tax advice for an individual case.
