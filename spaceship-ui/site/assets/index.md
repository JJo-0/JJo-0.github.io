# Park JiHo | AI & Robotics Research Notes

Park JiHo's personal technical site connects robotics, artificial intelligence, computer vision, machine vision, human-robot interaction, safety, learning, and system integration. The work focuses on intelligent robotic systems that perceive, reason, and act around people, with an emphasis on implementation and experimental verification rather than concept-only descriptions.

## Research

The public research map is organized around connected research axes rather than isolated demos. It covers perception and vision, human-robot interaction and safety, learning and intelligent behavior, and the systems work required to integrate sensing, estimation, software, simulation, control, and robotic hardware into a working pipeline.
