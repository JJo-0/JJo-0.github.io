Three evidence layers

1. Behavior

Self-reports, avoidance behavior, metacognition, and linguistic consistency.

2. Mechanism

Global sharing, recurrence, higher-order representations, self-models, and welfare-like representations.

3. Phenomenology

Qualia, sentience, and actual subjective experience: what it feels like.

Key point: Stronger evidence at layers 1 and 2 does not automatically establish layer 3.

The central divide: Substrate independence

Computational Functionalism

Prioritizes causal/information-processing roles over material. Artificial consciousness is possible in principle if the required structures can be implemented in silicon.

Biological Naturalism

Treats metabolism, homeostasis, and embodied self-maintenance as necessary life processes. Distinguishes functional simulation from experiential instantiation.
