HOW THE INDUSTRY FITS TOGETHER

Six overlapping layers connect the semiconductor industry.

Demand sets chip specifications. Design, wafers, and front-end processes turn them into chips. Packaging and testing complete the product. Materials, equipment, critical components, and fab infrastructure support each of these stages.
