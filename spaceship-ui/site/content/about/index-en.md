## 🔬 Research and projects

### Human–robot safety monitoring

Master's research · Korea Institute of Industrial Technology and Sungkyunkwan University

- Studied methods for updating protective separation distances around robots using human joint positions obtained from multiple RGB-D cameras.
- Constructed safety zones incorporating human approach velocity toward individual robot links and perception uncertainty.
- Replayed recorded data from a UR5e environment to compare protective separation distance and robot-stop ratios against a fixed-reference approach.

### Potato-preprocessing automation · Unpeeled-area recognition

Collaborative KITECH project · Image collection and labeling; DeepLabV3

- Collected images and constructed segmentation labels distinguishing peeled and unpeeled regions on potato surfaces.
- Trained a DeepLabV3 segmentation model and reviewed predictions of remaining unpeeled regions.

### Seafood-processing automation · Fish-part recognition

Collaborative KITECH project · SAM-Adapter and DeepLabV3+

- Conducted image-segmentation research to distinguish major parts, including the head and body, in tuna-processing images.
- Used SAM-Adapter and compared and validated predictions against an existing segmentation model.

### Autonomous transport robot for small factories · Graduation project

March 2023–March 2024 · Four-person team · ROS2, Docker, PyQt5, Python, C++

- Designed hardware and the lift's electrical system for a cart-type robot supporting factory transport and working-height adjustment.
- Built a Docker development environment and tuned ROS2 navigation parameters for confined spaces.
- Developed a GUI for lift-height settings, manual control, and robot-status monitoring.
