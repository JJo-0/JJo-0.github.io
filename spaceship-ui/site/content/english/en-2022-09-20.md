### **Why I chose GitHub**

---

1. **A suggestion from my roommate**

I used to write on Naver Blog. It was convenient, but I had little control over the format.
I wanted to make something that felt like my own.
Building a website would have given me the most freedom, but at the time that felt beyond me.
I kept putting the blog off until my roommate, a software engineering student,
asked, “Want me to build it?” A day later, here it was.

2. **Learning about the web and Git**

I had imagined building a website from scratch. A GitHub blog gave me a manageable place to start.
There were things I wanted to try that meant learning GitHub along the way. That sounded fun,
so I decided to start writing here.

---

### **About the blog**

---

- ⏰ **Posting schedule
Two posts a week, on Tuesdays and Thursdays**

I'd love to publish every day, but I also want to give each post the time it deserves.
I'm still getting used to writing, and a single post takes quite a bit of work.
I'll start with this schedule and aim to publish more often as writing becomes easier.
- **💿 What I'll write about
Classes, independent study, and music**

For classes at school and lessons I follow online, including on YouTube, I plan to post PDFs
and type up my own notes on the material.
I'd also like to write about what I learn on my own,
especially programming and building this blog.
