            </svg>

        Biochemical mechanisms: Relaxation and energy metabolism
    </h3>
    <p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-6">

    </p>

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6">

            Sleep and stress regulation: The original relaxation account
        </h4>
        <p class="text-blue-800 text-sm leading-relaxed mb-3">

        </p>
        <div class="text-xs text-blue-700 bg-blue-100 rounded-lg p-3">
            <strong>Study described in the original:</strong> 500mg daily for eight weeks, with reported improvements in sleep duration/efficiency, melatonin, and cortisol. This is a study description, not a self-dosing instruction.
        </div>
    </div>

<div class="bg-amber-50 rounded-xl p-6 border-l-4 border-amber-400">
