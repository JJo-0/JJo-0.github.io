This was a clinical trial in people. Its two stages addressed different questions.
Phase 1b examined the combination dose and early safety. Phase 2 looked for evidence of tumor shrinkage.

- <strong>Biliary tract cancer (BTC)</strong> covers cancers arising in the bile ducts or gallbladder.
- <strong>HER2-positive</strong> means that cancer cells show high HER2 protein expression or amplification of the corresponding gene.
- <strong>Trastuzumab</strong> is an antibody targeting HER2.
- <strong>Nivolumab</strong> is an antibody that blocks the PD-1 immune checkpoint to support an immune response.
- <strong>Gemcitabine and cisplatin</strong> are cytotoxic chemotherapy drugs used together in first-line BTC treatment.
- <strong>Single arm</strong> means every participant received the same investigational regimen. Earlier studies provide context,

37 phase 2 participants, 40 in total. At data cutoff, 12 remained on treatment and 28 had stopped.
Progression accounted for 26 discontinuations and adverse events for two. Read this flow diagram

## 1. A substantial response signal in a study without a comparator

Published September 22, 2026 in *Nature Medicine*, HERBOT examined first-line treatment of
unresectable advanced HER2-positive biliary tract cancer with trastuzumab, nivolumab, gemcitabine and cisplatin.
This phase 1b/2, open-label, single-arm trial enrolled 40 participants at 12 Korean institutions. The checkpoint inhibitor was

<strong>nivolumab</strong>. The published regimen did not include durvalumab.
<a href="#news-ref-1" data-news-citation="1" aria-label="Go to reference 1" data-astro-reload>
  [1]
</a>

One complete and 21 partial responses gave 22 objective responses and an objective response rate (ORR) of 55%
(95% CI 38.5–70.7%). Including stable disease gave a disease control rate (DCR) of 95%. Median duration of response
was 12.6 months. At 17.0 months of median follow-up, median progression-free survival (PFS) was 10.6 months.
Median overall survival (OS) had not been reached. Two people underwent curative-intent conversion surgery.<a href="#news-ref-1" data-news-citation="1" aria-label="Go to reference 1" data-astro-reload>[1]</a>

The main limitation is the absence of a contemporaneous randomized control. The 55% is the proportion of the 40 participants
who had a complete or partial RECIST response. Participants were selected for HER2 positivity,
72.5% had gallbladder cancer, and most had ECOG performance status 1. Differences from historical trials can reflect
the patient mix, assessment schedule, length of follow-up and the drug combination. These findings support a phase 3 study.
Replacing standard treatment would require direct comparative evidence.

  cancer location and stage, organ function, risk of toxicity, regulatory approval and reimbursement, in consultation with the treating team.

## 2. Why combine four drugs?

Gemcitabine and cisplatin are chemotherapies that damage rapidly dividing cancer cells. Nivolumab blocks PD-1 signaling,
partly relieving a brake on the T-cell response to cancer. Trastuzumab targets cells expressing high levels of HER2.
The team hypothesized that targeting different pathways together in the first line might produce
deeper and more durable responses.

A single treatment arm cannot separate the effects of the four drugs. There was no randomized arm that omitted
trastuzumab or nivolumab. To separate the contributions from HER2 targeting, immunotherapy, chemotherapy and patient selection
would require comparative groups. Synergy is a biologically motivated hypothesis. This study
could not establish a statistical interaction among the drugs.

The three phase 1b participants received trastuzumab at an 8 mg/kg loading dose followed by 6 mg/kg, with
nivolumab 360 mg on day 1, gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m² and cisplatin 25 mg/m² on days 1 and 8 of each three-week cycle.
No dose-limiting toxicity occurred, so the starting doses became the recommended phase 2 doses. The planned maximum
was nine cycles of cytotoxic chemotherapy, with trastuzumab and nivolumab permitted for up to two years.<a href="#news-ref-1" data-news-citation="1" aria-label="Go to reference 1" data-astro-reload>[1]</a>

The absence of dose-limiting toxicity in three people gives limited information on rare events.
Among all 40 patients, grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events included neutropenia in 57.5%, anemia in 30.0%
and thrombocytopenia in 22.5%. The frequency of these hematologic toxicities belongs alongside the response rate.

## 3. Reading Figure 2: one bar per patient

<NewsFigure media="sep23-herbot-fig-2" lang="en" />

Figure 2a is a waterfall plot. Each bar represents one participant, and the vertical axis gives the greatest percentage change
from baseline in the sum of target-lesion diameters. Negative values indicate shrinkage. The dashed line at −30% relates to the
RECIST size threshold for partial response. The two red bars above +20% point toward progression. The dark navy bar at the far
right indicates a complete response. Strips beneath the bars identify cancer subtype and HER2 test status.

Figure 2b adds time in a spider plot. Each line follows one patient's tumor size over several months.
Some tumors initially shrank and then grew again, while some remained smaller. A patient's target lesions can also remain
stable while new lesions appear elsewhere. The single lowest point on a waterfall plot therefore cannot establish
durability. Duration of response and PFS provide additional information.

<CandidateEquation lang="en" id="herbot-orr" title="Which patients form the numerator and denominator of the 55% ORR?" tex={String.raw`\widehat p_{\mathrm{ORR}}=\frac{n_{\mathrm{CR}}+n_{\mathrm{PR}}}{N}=\frac{1+21}{40}=0.55\tag{1}`}>

<Math tex={String.raw`N`} /> is the 40 participants in the efficacy analysis. <Math tex={String.raw`n_{\mathrm{CR}}`} /> counts the one
complete response with disappearance of all target lesions. <Math tex={String.raw`n_{\mathrm{PR}}`} /> counts the 21 partial
responses. Dividing their total of 22 by 40 gives 0.55, or 55%. This dimensionless
sample statistic estimates a population proportion.

Observing 22 responders does not establish that exactly 55% of a larger group would respond under comparable conditions.
The reported 95% confidence interval of 38.5–70.7% shows the uncertainty with 40 participants. The lower
limit is above zero, supporting a response signal, while the full interval spans about 32 percentage points.
Reporting only 55% would overstate precision.

DCR includes complete responses, partial responses and stable disease in its numerator. It reached 95% because it also
includes temporarily stable tumors. ORR and DCR answer different questions about tumor shrinkage
and the proportion whose best response did not involve progression.

</CandidateEquation>

## 4. Why 55% does not directly measure an advantage over standard care

A single-arm trial lacks a comparator assessed simultaneously under the same eligibility criteria. Subtracting a response
rate from a different trial and calling the result an improvement overlooks differences in HER2 status, gallbladder cancer prevalence,
performance status, scan intervals, follow-up and subsequent treatment. These can all contribute to a cross-trial difference.

A randomized trial could assign patients with the same eligibility criteria to existing chemoimmunotherapy
or the HER2-targeted four-drug regimen and compare PFS and OS. Blinded independent central review,
stratification, prespecified subgroups and adequate enrollment could reduce selection bias and time-related differences.

## 5. Interpreting the steps and crosses in Figure 3

<NewsFigure media="sep23-herbot-fig-3" lang="en" />

Figure 3a shows a Kaplan–Meier curve for PFS. A step down marks radiographic progression or death.
Small crosses indicate censoring. The person was event-free up to that time, after which the outcome became unknown
or follow-up ended at data cutoff. A cross does not signify a cure. The numbers at risk below the graph count participants
under observation without an event just before each time point. Fewer people contribute later, making the tail more uncertain.

Figure 3b shows OS. Estimated 12-month survival was 72.7% (95% CI 55.0–84.4%). Median OS was NR, or not reached,
because the Kaplan–Meier estimate had not fallen to the 50% threshold by data cutoff.
This can be encouraging but leaves long-term survival uncertain.

<CandidateEquation lang="en" id="herbot-km" title="Why does a Kaplan–Meier curve descend in steps?" tex={String.raw`\widehat S(t)=\prod_{t_i\le t}\left(1-\frac{d_i}{n_i}\right)\tag{2}`}>

<Math tex={String.raw`\widehat S(t)`} /> estimates the probability of remaining event-free through time t.
<Math tex={String.raw`t_i`} /> is an event time. <Math tex={String.raw`d_i`} /> is the number of
events at that time.
<Math tex={String.raw`n_i`} /> is the number still in the risk set immediately beforehand. The estimate holds between
events and drops when a new conditional survival fraction enters the product.

For example, if one of ten people under observation progresses, the conditional survival fraction at that time is <Math tex={String.raw`1-1/10=0.9`} />. If one of eight at risk progresses at the next event time, cumulative estimated survival becomes <Math tex={String.raw`0.9\times(1-1/8)=0.7875`} />. This hypothetical calculation illustrates the equation without reanalyzing individual HERBOT records.

Censoring removes a participant from the risk set without counting an event. The analysis assumes that censoring is
independent of future event risk. If people who deteriorate are more likely to leave follow-up, the curve could look overly
favorable. The 95% CI and numbers at risk help readers interpret the whole curve and the density of follow-up.

</CandidateEquation>

## 6. What does the AI analysis in Figure 4 establish?

<NewsFigure media="sep23-herbot-fig-4" lang="en" />

Figure 4a shows AI assessment of HER2 staining and the tumor microenvironment in whole-slide pathology images.
The system classified cells on HER2 IHC as 0, 1+, 2+ or 3+. On H&E slides, it distinguished tumor and stroma and located lymphocytes
to derive immune-inflamed, immune-excluded and immune-desert phenotypes. Central pathology using
IHC/ISH criteria determined enrollment. The AI work was
a planned exploratory analysis.

In Figure 4b the 15 participants with at least 10% HER2 3+ tumor cells on whole-slide assessment had an ORR of 80%.
The other 25 had an ORR of 40%. Panel c compares PFS, and panels d and e examine immune phenotype and PFS.
The swimmer plot in f places treatment, survival and pathology features together on a row for each person.
These associations identify candidate biomarkers for further study.

The subgroups of 15 and 25 are small, and the study made multiple exploratory comparisons. Validation should test
the 10% cutoff as a prespecified criterion in an independent cohort and across scanners, staining conditions and model versions.
Predicting a treatment interaction also requires measuring the biomarker in a control arm.
Prognostic differences within one arm cannot establish that the AI predicts benefit from trastuzumab.

<CandidateEquation lang="en" id="herbot-hr" title="What a hazard ratio of 0.57 measures" tex={String.raw`\mathrm{HR}(t)=\frac{h_1(t)}{h_0(t)}\tag{3}`}>

<Math tex={String.raw`h_1(t)`} /> and <Math tex={String.raw`h_0(t)`} /> are the instantaneous hazards of an event among people who have
remained event-free through time t. The exploratory Cox model for the HER2 3+ proportion groups in Figure 4c
reported HR 0.57 with a wide 95% CI of 0.23–1.34. The 0.57 is a model-based ratio of instantaneous hazards.
It is not a percentage surviving.

For example, an instantaneous hazard of 0.10 in the reference group and 0.057 in the comparison group gives a ratio of 0.57.
Cumulative survival depends on the hazard over time. The interval includes 1, so
these data do not rule out equal hazards. The p-value for ORR in Figure 4b and
the PFS interval in Figure 4c therefore answer different questions.

The analysis quantified HER2 staining and the spatial immune environment on pathology slides.
It establishes hypotheses for a validation trial.

</CandidateEquation>

## 7. Reading safety and conflicts of interest alongside efficacy

Tumor shrinkage must be weighed against receiving four drugs. Grade 3 or higher neutropenia in 57.5%, anemia in 30.0%
and thrombocytopenia in 22.5% indicate frequent severe cytopenias. Infections, bleeding, dose delays, dose reductions and
supportive care also determine feasibility. A high response rate tells us little by itself about
how tolerable treatment is in practice.

The study included 12 hospitals and used central HER2 testing. Interpretation also depends on the open-label, single-arm design,
the small cohort, the 72.5% proportion with gallbladder cancer and the generalizability of a Korean-only sample.
The paper's funding and competing-interest statements identify support and author interests, including company researchers
involved in AI pathology. These disclosures make adherence to protocol and independent replication especially important.

The trial registration NCT05749900 and Supplementary Protocol help readers check which endpoints were defined in advance,
the continuation rule in the Simon two-stage design and what analysis was planned after data cutoff.
Nine of the first 16 participants had objective responses, meeting the prespecified
criterion for continuing to the next stage.<a href="#news-ref-2" data-news-citation="2" aria-label="Go to reference 2" data-astro-reload>[2]</a>

## 8. What would change the interpretation?

A randomized phase 3 trial is the most important next test. Does the four-drug regimen improve blinded-review PFS and OS
relative to a control in the same first-line setting, using the same HER2 definition? Dose intensity, discontinuation,
hospitalization and quality of life also matter. The 10% HER2 3+ threshold and immune phenotypes need validation in other cohorts
and on other scanners. Future papers can also show whether planned endpoints still unreported, including ctDNA,
are consistent with the initial findings.

Four questions help interpret HERBOT. What phase was it? Was there a randomized control? Who formed the denominator for response?
How many remained at risk near the tail of the survival curve? This single-arm phase 1b/2 study in 40 people found encouraging
response and PFS signals. Severe hematologic toxicity was common, a direct comparator was absent, and the AI biomarker was exploratory.

<strong>First-line HER2 targeting combined with immunotherapy and chemotherapy produced a substantial response signal
in 40 patients with advanced HER2-positive biliary tract cancer. A randomized comparison is now needed to quantify the
additional benefit and burden.</strong>
