## Evidence and interpretation

- ELPR: Nature DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-11042-0 and its supplement, published September 16. The 110/160 nm line/space result is fluorescent EBL patterning; 30 micrometres is the working multicolour OLED pixel diameter. The 13.3% figure describes the directly patternable material, whereas 30.3% in the article text/caption describes the precursor. Figure 5a prints 30.9%; neither image nor caption has been silently rewritten. Figure 5c's current-density axis differs from its caption wording. The article explains the plotted axis. Figure 4n's pitch wording differs from the line/space description; 270 nm is an explicitly calculated ideal period, not a new resolution measurement.
- ELPR lifetime: the main text calls the improved 146-minute device crosslinked, but Supplementary Figure 63D calls it non-crosslinked. The displayed drive is 2.4 mA/cm2, unlike the initial approximately 70-minute case at 1 mA/cm2. The article preserves this discrepancy and does not claim a verified doubling of crosslinked-device lifetime. The actual supplementary page was visually inspected. Patent EP25223816.7 is attributed.
- Xenocortication: full public HTML, original Figures 2/3/4 and their captions. The very large publisher PDF exceeded the acquisition size bound and is not represented as obtained. The 91.9% denominator is graft plus residual cortex, not whole-brain volume. Animal counts, cell/nucleus counts, growth and survival are separate. The Y-maze significance against chance is not represented as a between-group comparison. Functional activity and partial behaviour changes are not human cognition or normalized behaviour.
- Anthropic: the September 17 official article, technical report and official optimization-kit README. Exact/Fast/Big forward-pass summaries cover 14/13/14 configurations, not identical sets of 36 models. One extreme-input node contains eight B300 GPUs. Executed but collapsed structures are shown beside performance and quality graphs. Reference reproduction, numerical agreement, structural quality and wet-lab validation are separate. The release is described as unmaintained reference tooling.
- Hydrogen: Nature Energy DOI 10.1038/s41560-026-02126-2, September 17. The 14,108 entries are potential offtakers. The historical natural-gas evidence is an analogue used in simulations, not a hydrogen policy trial. The controlled 37–41% and EHB-like 13–15% changes concern long-horizon cumulative cost effectiveness through 2100. Toy budgets illustrate denominators and are not inferred real budgets.
